NP-VerbAlgorithmic reverb

A reverb you can automate while it plays.

NP-Verb is a modulated feedback-delay-network with its own early-reflection stage. Changing size, pre-delay or decay during playback cross-fades between settings instead of bending the pitch, so it is safe to automate on a live vocal. Freeze holds the tail forever.

Questions

Does the suite add latency?

NP-Zero, NP-EQ and NP-Verb are zero-latency. NP-Gate, NP-DeEss, NP-Comp and NP-Limit use look-ahead and report it to the host, so your DAW compensates automatically when tracking or mixing.

Where are presets stored?

Factory presets are built in, organised by genre and task. Your own presets are saved as files in %APPDATA%\NullPointAudio\<plugin>\Presets, so they survive reinstalls and can be copied between machines.

Can I use them one at a time?

Yes. The chain order is a recommendation. Each plugin is an ordinary VST3 effect and can go anywhere on any track, vocal or not.

How do I uninstall?

Windows Settings → Apps → Null Point Audio NP Vocal Suite → Uninstall. Your presets and skin setting are kept unless you delete the NullPointAudio folder yourself.

macOS?

Not yet. The suite is Windows-only for version 1.0.
